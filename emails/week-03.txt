Subject: Session 03 is open — your message

This is the session your niche was chosen for.

Session 03 — Message is now open. By the end of it you will be clear on three things: who you serve, what they are struggling with, and the transformation you offer.

The shape of every session is the same, so you always know what you are walking into: the video first — it walks the Working Sheet with you, section by section — then the sheet itself, brought rough rather than finished. We complete it together on the call. This week that is 7 min to watch and 60 min for the sheet: about 65 minutes. Your list runs in the background on top of that.

Most of the sheet is one job — understanding the person you want to help. Their pain points, and what is actually going wrong for them now. The outcome they want instead. The obstacles in the way of getting there, including everything they have already tried. And the cost of changing nothing.

Get those right and two things follow. Your messaging resonates, because you are saying their own situation back to them more clearly than they can say it themselves. And your coaching program gets easier to build — you know exactly what it has to solve, so nothing goes into it that does not earn its place. That is Session 04, and it starts here.

One thing before you open the sheet: go and read how those people talk about their own lives. Forums, comment threads, reviews — anywhere they were talking to each other rather than to a professional. Then write what you found into section two, in your own words.

Quantity first, editing second. Ten lines under each heading, and everything down before you judge any of it.

Your announcement goes out at the end of our next session — one message, to everyone on your list. You draft it in section four and we finish it together, so there is nothing to write alone.
