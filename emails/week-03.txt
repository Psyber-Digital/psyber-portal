Subject: Session 03 is open — your message

A niche is the decision everything else is built on. With that settled, the next question is how you say it.

Session 03 — Message is now open. It starts with the oldest structure in storytelling: your client is the hero and you are the guide. Gandalf does not carry the ring. That distinction shapes every word you write from here.

The shape of every session is the same, so you always know what you are walking into: the video first — it walks the Working Sheet with you, section by section — then the sheet itself, brought rough rather than finished. We complete it together on the call. This week that is 7 min to watch and 60 min for the sheet: about 65 minutes. Your list runs in the background on top of that.

One sheet this week, four sections — and it is the one you keep. Your website words, your emails and your program's own wording all come out of it for years, so treat it as a document you return to rather than something to finish once.

The four run in the order the message gets built. First the high level and your one-sentence coaching statement. Then what is true for them — seven headings, lettered A to G, and this is where most of the hour goes. Then a short story about one client, ten minutes, which is the fastest way to hear whether the first two hang together. Then your outreach.

One thing to do before you start: go and read what the people you want to serve say about their own lives — forums, comment threads, reviews, anywhere they were talking to each other rather than to a professional. Then write what you found into section two, in your own words. Everything else is easier once you have read them rather than guessed at them.

Aim for ten lines under each heading. Put everything down before you judge any of it, then edit.

Practice the therapy question out loud before we speak — how is this different from therapy. It should come out at the same speed as your own name.

One thing to know about our next session: your announcement goes out at the end of it. Section four of the sheet is where you draft it, along with the two follow-ups that come after — rough is fine, we finish them together on the call. Write them in your own voice rather than a template's. The people on your list already know how you talk.
