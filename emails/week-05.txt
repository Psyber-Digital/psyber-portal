Subject: Session 05 is open — we build this one together

You have a map and a price. This session we build the first session of your program — together, on the call, rather than sending you away to make something.

Here is why. This is the point where people stop: it is a blank page, and it is the first thing you will make that a real person will see. So we remove both. There is a slide template with the gaps already marked, we fill it in on the call, and we send you the file afterwards.

The shape of every session is the same, so you always know what you are walking into: the video first — it walks the Working Sheet with you, block by block — then the sheet itself, brought rough rather than finished. We complete it together on the call. This week that is 5 min to watch and 15 min rough first pass: about 20 minutes. Your list runs in the background on top of that.

One difference this week: the Working Sheet and the slide template mirror each other, block for slide, so fill whichever you prefer and the other one fills in. Put a rough first thought in each box. Four scruffy lines is a good outcome. Please do not polish, and please do not open a design tool.

One thing to read before we speak. There is a minimum standard — one objective, three to five key points, one exercise, one action — and once you clear it you are finished. Not when it feels finished. When it clears the floor.
