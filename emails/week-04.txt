Subject: Session 04 is open — structure and offer

Your announcement is out, which means you have told people what you are building. That is a bigger step than it sounds, and most people never take it.

Session 04 — Structure & Offer is now open. Two things get decided: the shape of your program, and the price.

The shape of every session is the same, so you always know what you are walking into: the video first — it walks the Working Sheet with you, block by block — then the sheet itself, brought rough rather than finished. We complete it together on the call. This week that is 8 min to watch and 35 min for the sheet: about 45 minutes. Your list runs in the background on top of that.

One instruction that will save you a fortnight. The map is one page — modules, the job each one does, and how many weeks. That is all that is needed to sell. If you find yourself writing unit titles or listing exercises, that is a level deeper than this session needs.

The video also covers the four ways to make an offer stronger, and how to set your two numbers — including building the action-taking discount in from the start rather than discounting on the spot.
