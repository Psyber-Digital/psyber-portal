Subject: Session 02 is open — your niche

Good first session. Your foundations are in place, which is exactly the ground we needed before this.

Session 02 — Niche is now open, and it carries more pre-work than most sessions. That is deliberate: generating and researching are things you can do alone, and deciding is not. Deciding is what our session is for.

The shape of every session is the same, so you always know what you are walking into: the video first — it walks the Working Sheet with you, block by block — then the sheet itself, brought rough rather than finished. We complete it together on the call. This week that is 7 min to watch, 30 min generating and 30 min researching three: about 65 minutes.

Try to do the stages in separate sittings. Generate everything first and judge none of it; narrow it down later, ideally after a night’s gap. Ideas settle when you leave them alone, and the second sitting usually produces something the first could not reach.

Bring three to the session, with what your research turned up. Do not pick — we pick together, and then move straight on.

Nothing goes out this week, so there is nothing to brace for. You need the words before you can tell anyone, and that is the next session — your announcement goes out at the end of it.
