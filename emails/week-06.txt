Subject: Session 06 is open — the conversation

You have a first session built, which means you have something real to describe rather than something you intend to make. That changes how the next conversation goes.

Session 06 — The Conversation is now open. The video walks the discovery call from start to finish, in order, and your Working Sheet follows the same sequence so you can write your own version of each step.

The shape of every session is the same, so you always know what you are walking into: the video first — it walks the Working Sheet with you, block by block — then the sheet itself, brought rough rather than finished. We complete it together on the call. This week that is 8 min to watch, 25 min for the sheet and 15 min out loud: about 50 minutes.

Start with the video, because it opens with something worth knowing: the data on what actually converts describes a clinical assessment almost exactly. You come to this with most of the skill already in place.

Most of our session is rehearsal. We practice saying the price and staying quiet, and the two questions that only come up for therapists. Come ready to say things out loud, and bring any real conversations you have had — especially ones that went badly.
