Subject: Session 06 is open — your offer and the conversation

The first session of your program is ready. Now you turn your program into an offer, and you practice the conversation that turns interest into a client.

Your offer is what somebody buys: what changes for them, what's included, and the price. The conversation is a discovery call, where you both find out whether you can help them.

Session 06 — Offer & Conversation is now open.

Watch the video first, then fill in the sheet: seven steps, in two sittings with a break between. Sitting one builds your offer and your price. Sitting two builds your call guide, your slides if you use video calls, and your answers to common concerns, and you say it all out loud. Start at least three days before our next call or weekly drop-in. Less than three days to go? Do what you can in one sitting. Send what you have at least 24 hours before a call, or bring it to the drop-in. Then we go through it together and practice the conversation.

Keep your conversations going. When somebody says your program sounds like them, offer them a discovery call. If it's a former therapy client, thank them, and ask who they know instead. If it's a current therapy client, don't offer coaching and don't ask who they know. Your work with them stays as therapy.

Before your first discovery call, check your state board's rules, and set up payment and a coaching calendar. Step 4 of your Working Sheet shows what to check.
