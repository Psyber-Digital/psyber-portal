Structure & Offer

04 · Structure, Offer & Pricing
Until perceived value exceeds price, you should never reveal the price.

ONE PAGE, AND EVERY MODULE HAS A JOB

Modules, in order, with a total length. Beside each one, write its job: either a clear objective the client reaches, or a specific fear it removes. A module that does neither is a topic rather than a step, and it can come out.

SLIP — THE TEST FOR EVERY ELEMENT

Simple · Lean · Impactful · Practical. A short program a client finishes beats a thorough one they abandon.

THE BUILD, STOPPED EARLY

Transformation as a question → the questions that must be answered → cut what is not necessary → sequence by how change happens → module titles → the job of each → duration. Module one is always mindset. Aim for 8–12 weeks. Stop there.

THE EVIDENCE ON LENGTH

The best available evidence does not show longer coaching producing better outcomes. A short program is a design decision.

WHAT PEOPLE ARE ACTUALLY BUYING

Transformative resultsWhat changes, not what is included. People buy the change and justify it with the features
Effort and time savedEighteen months of trial and error, or ten weeks with the wrong turns removed. You are selling the shortcut as well as the destination

FOUR WAYS TO STRENGTHEN AN OFFER — PICK AT LEAST ONE

ScarcityA genuine limit. “I am taking five clients at this price” — and mean it
UrgencyA reason to decide now. The action-taking price, on the call or within 24 hours
High-value bonusesLow effort for you, high value for them. A spreadsheet, a short guide, a planner
Risk reversalConditional guarantee: complete the work start to end, and if you are not satisfied, a full refund

YOUR TWO NUMBERS

A founding price for your first three clients — not less than $1,500 — and the price after that, roughly double. The gap is an exchange, not a discount. After every three clients at a price, it goes up. Three clients, then you move.

BUILD THE ACTION-TAKING DISCOUNT IN

Set your number, then add $500. On the call, offer $500 off for a decision within 48 hours. The people who act tend to be the people who finish, so you are rewarding the behavior that predicts their success — and pricing it in from the start rather than discounting on the spot.

THE RULE THAT GOVERNS PRICING

Never give anyone a price until they understand the value. The number means nothing on its own; it only means something next to what they are getting.
