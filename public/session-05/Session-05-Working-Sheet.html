Therapy+ · Working Sheet · Session 05

Creation

Create only what is necessary to move forward.
Six steps, numbered as in the video, in two sittings with a break between. They build your first session: its slides, your notes, its worksheet and its Principle Card. Every step has a worked example: the coach for tech start-up founders from your Session 04 examples. Start at least three days before our next call. Aim for steps one to five. Step six comes after you deliver your first session. If time runs short, the floor is enough: one objective, three to five key points, one exercise, one action. On the call, we go through what you've built, together.
Sitting 1 of 2 · steps 1 to 3 · your first session · about an hour, plus a quick run-through out loud

01The big picture

Open your Session 04 sheet to your modules, their outcomes (step 5) and their key ideas (step 10). Then write your client's journey in two or three lines: where they start, how each module moves them on, and where they finish.
Example. Founders start out working every evening and still feeling behind. First they plan each week around their three most important outcomes. Then their tools protect two focused blocks a day, and they hold one clear boundary between work and home. They finish with a daily routine that keeps them steady through a hard week, and their evenings back.

02Your first session's slides

Build only your first session now. The rest of your program waits until you've delivered it. If a slide won't come, ask yourself: knowing everything I know, what would I say to somebody going through this? And if it were happening to me, what would I do?
A slide for each part of your first session. Show them in your session, or use them as your notes.
  1. Pick your colors, then download. The file opens in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides.
  2. Change each slide, in order. Every box says what goes there.
  3. Title and objective: your Module 1 name and its outcome, from Session 04 step 5. Transformation: the second half of your Session 04 step 1 question, written to your client, starting “You…”.
  4. Key points: we recommend three for a first session, already named on the slides. The Blueprint: what your program looks like and covers. The Required Mindset: the beliefs your client needs. Success Factors: what raises their chances. Under each, put that unit's key idea from Session 04 step 10. Change any of them if your session needs to.
  5. Exercise: one thing your client does in the session. Action items: they book their next session, then do one piece of work before it, on a day they name.
  6. For a fourth key point, copy a key point slide. To show one in parts, drag in a spare slide from the end of the file. Delete the spares you don't use.
  7. Read it through once. You're done when your objective, key points, exercise and action are in your own words. That's the floor. No slides? Email those four to your coach instead.

03Your speaking points

In the notes under each slide, write a few words to guide you, never a script. In Keynote, choose View, then Show Presenter Notes. Then run through your session out loud once. We practice it together on our next call.
Sitting 2 of 2 · steps 4 to 6 · your worksheet and card · about 45 minutes

04Your worksheet

One page your client fills in. Ours is only for inspiration: use any format you like.
  1. Download it. It opens in Word or Pages, in your slides' colors.
  2. Insert a title, and one line on what it's for.
  3. Turn your exercise into a question, and your action into another.
  4. Add a hint wherever your client might get stuck. Size each box to its answer, and delete anything you don't need.

05Your Principle Card

One page your client keeps. Ours is only for inspiration: use any format you like, or another one-page resource. Keep it a coaching page, never a handout from your therapy work.
  1. Download it. It opens in Word or Pages, in your slides' colors.
  2. At the top, insert the one line you want your client to remember.
  3. Then your key points, in your client's words, with one line to explain each.
  4. Add one thing more only if it helps the transformation. Delete anything you don't need.

06Build just in time

After you deliver your first session to your first client, come back and note how it went. Those notes shape your second session, which you build in full only then. Spare time before then? Rough out session two in box B. Keep your coaching files in their own folder, separate from your therapy records.
A · After you deliver your first session · leave blank for now
Example. What landed · the five modules on one page; they asked to keep a copy. What didn't · the missed-week plan sounded like a rule, not a choice. What needed more explaining · how the twelve weeks are spaced. What was unnecessary · asking which module they're most looking forward to; they'd already said. What resonated · "balance is a skill." Where they glazed over · the long walk through all five modules.
