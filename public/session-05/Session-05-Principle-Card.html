05 · Program Creation
Create only what is necessary to move forward.

BUILD AS YOU GO

A clear structure and a strong first session are all you need to start. By the end of this session, your first session has its slides, your notes, its worksheet and its Principle Card.

JUST-IN-TIME COACHING PROGRAM CREATION

What it isBuild only your first session in full now. Once you have a client, deliver it, then build the next session from how it went. Keep the rest rough, and fill each one in just in time
It gets you startedIt stops you from procrastinating
It makes your program betterYou know exactly what the next session needs, because you've just seen how the last one went

THE SIX STEPS, IN TWO SITTINGS

1 · The big pictureYour client's journey, in two or three lines
2 · Your first session's slidesYour first session only. Change each slide: title · transformation · objective · key points · exercise · action items
3 · Your speaking pointsA few words in the notes under each slide. Say the session out loud once
4 · Your worksheetOne page your client fills in. Every box something they do
5 · Your Principle CardOne line to remember, then your key points
6 · Build just in timeBuild each session after delivering the one before, shaped by how that one went

THREE KEY POINTS WE RECOMMEND FOR A FIRST SESSION

The BlueprintWhat your program looks like and what it covers
The Required MindsetThe beliefs your client needs to make it work
Success FactorsWhat raises their chances of reaching the transformation
Your callChange any of them if your first session needs something different

THE FLOOR: CLEAR IT AND STOP

One objectiveIn a single sentence
Three to five key pointsUsually one per unit
One exerciseSomething they do in the session
One actionWhat they leave with

TWO QUESTIONS FOR A SLIDE THAT WON'T COME

Knowing everything I know……what would I say to somebody going through this?
If it were happening to me……what would I do about it?

AFTER EACH SESSION YOU DELIVER

Note how it went. What landed, and what didn't. What needed more explaining, and what was unnecessary. What resonated, and where your client glazed over.

KEEP IT SIMPLE

A few points per slide. Practical, not theoretical. Only resources your client uses. Done beats perfect.

BUILD · DELIVER · IMPROVE

Use your research, experience and expertise to create something you can deliver. Then deliver it, and let your clients show you how to make it better.
