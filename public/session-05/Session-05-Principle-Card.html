Creation

05 · Fulfilment
Good enough shipped beats great unshipped. Don't build anything without first validating profitability.

WHAT YOUR FIRST SESSION IS FOR

Welcome themThey have just spent real money on a decision they were circling. Tell them it was a good call
Onboard themHow often you meet, how long, what happens between, how they reach you, what you need from them
Show them the mapModules in order and what changes at the end of each. People relax when they can see the road
Give them the success factorsWhat makes clients succeed here, and what tends to derail them. A gift on day one

WHAT DOES NOT BELONG IN SESSION ONE

Deep content. Frameworks. Your best material. Trying to fit those in is what makes a first session hard to build. Ease them in and let them leave glad they started.

THE FLOOR — CLEAR IT AND STOP

One objectiveIn a single sentence
Three to five key pointsNo more
One exerciseSomething they actually do
One actionWhat they leave with

THAT IS A COACHING SESSION

It can be delivered from one page of notes. Slides are optional — some of the best sessions you will run are a worksheet and a conversation.

THE INSTINCT TO WATCH

The urge to add one more thing, and then one more, until it feels good enough to show someone. That instinct is the most expensive thing you will bring to this program.

REFINEMENT COMES FROM CLIENTS

Your first delivery will teach you more about this session than another month of preparing it. You improve it afterwards, based on what actually landed.

WHY WE BUILD IT NOW

It changes how you sound on a call — describing something that exists rather than something you intend to make. And it settles the worry that somebody says yes and you have nothing ready.
