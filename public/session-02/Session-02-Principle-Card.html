Niche

02 · Niche
Create a hypothesis and go to market; the market will show you the right way.

WHAT NICHING DOES FOR YOU

You serve rather than sellReaching them feels like recognition
You become the expertDepth reads as authority in a way breadth never does
You focusInstead of scattering across every possible client
Your message resonatesBecause it was written for somebody specific

THE THREE PARTS OF A GOOD NICHE

A specific group you could picture · a powerful pain point that keeps them awake · a clear before and after. Coaching is the bridge between the two.

WHY ANYONE HIRES A COACH

They cannot get there on their own · they want to get there faster · they want a proven system rather than figuring it out alone.

THE TWO FILTERS

A client with the need AND the means. And a big domain of life — health and wellbeing, wealth, relationships, and for some, purpose. Pain in a big domain is pain people pay to resolve.

THE FORMULA

I help [audience] achieve [transformation].

THE LANE TEST — RUN IT ON EVERY IDEA

Am I helping someone move from well to better, or am I treating something?

THE TRANSLATION BANK

Treat burnoutDesign a sustainable way of working
Manage anxietyPerform under pressure by design
Process griefRebuild a life and identity after a loss
Heal relationship traumaBuild the relationship you want next
Overcome low self-worthBuild the visible confidence to lead
Address perfectionismShip work at a pace you can sustain

THE FOUR CHECKS · 15 minutes each, top 3 only

GoogleAutocomplete, people-also-ask, and plenty of content around the idea
Reddit and forumsAn active thread in their own words
YouTubeReal view counts and engaged comments
Coaches and booksPeople already paying to solve this

GENERATE, THEN CULL — IN THAT ORDER

Write down more than feels sensible and judge none of it. Then leave it, and narrow in a later sitting. Judging while generating flattens the good ideas along with the weak ones.

TWO REFRAMES

Competition is proof — an empty field usually means no money. And you test it rather than marry it: the market corrects you faster than more thinking will.
