Niche

Create a hypothesis and go to market; the market will show you the right way.
Three stages, in order: generate everything, narrow it down, then research your top three. Finish each stage before moving on, and do them in separate sittings if you can. Do not pick a winner — we do that together on the call.

01Stage 1 · Generate — transformations

Changes you would be glad to deliver for the next three years. Write more than feels sensible, and judge none of it yet.
The transformation Why this one pulls at me

02Stage 1 · Generate — audiences

Groups that pull at you, and groups you have some real connection to — through your own life, your interests, or the people you already know.
The audience or group Why them — or how I know them

03Stage 2 · Narrow — your propositions

Pair up the strongest combinations, one line each. Then the lane check: are you moving somebody from well to better, or treating something?
I help ___ achieve ___ Well → better? (yes / no)

04Stage 2 · Narrow — score them

Out of ten, on three things only. Your top three by total score go to Stage 3.
Proposition They want it /10 They can pay /10 I can reach them /10
