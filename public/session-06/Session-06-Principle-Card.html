The Conversation

06 · Sales
A sale should be the natural outcome of a meaningful interaction. Serve, don't sell.

THE PRINCIPLE

A sale should be the natural outcome of a meaningful interaction. If the conversation is genuinely useful, the offer at the end is the obvious next step.

WHY YOU ARE ALREADY GOOD AT THIS

Analysis of hundreds of thousands of recorded sales calls: the highest converters ask 11–14 questions, talk slightly less than half the time, and go deep on three or four problems. That describes a clinical assessment.

THE RISK TO WATCH

Staying in assessment for the whole hour and never making an offer. Warmth is not the problem; the ending is.

THE DISCOVERY CALL, IN ORDER

1 · Set the agendaHow the call runs, how long it takes. You are holding it, not drifting
2 · Current situationWhere are they now, and what is it costing them
3 · Desired outcomeWhat they want instead, and what would change
4 · ObstaclesWhat has stopped them, and what they have already tried
5 · Cost of inactionWhere this goes in six months. Have them say it
6 · Are they a good fit?Answer honestly. If not, say so and point them somewhere better
7 · Introduce the programTied to what they said. Never a features list
8 · Show it worksWhy this handles the obstacle they described
9 · What they have by the endThe finish line, in their language
10 · Their questionsGenuinely let them. Silence is fine
11 · The priceWhen they ask, and not before
12 · Let them talkWhatever they say next is the most useful thing in the call

THE PRICE MOMENT

Say the number. Then stop talking. No justification, no softening. It feels enormous and it lasts about four seconds.

THE ACTION-TAKING DISCOUNT

$500 off for a decision within 48 hours — built into your price last session, so you are not cutting your own number on the spot. Explain why: people who decide quickly tend to do the work and get the result, and a decision left open for three weeks usually becomes no decision at all.

THE TWO QUESTIONS THAT CATCH THERAPISTS

How is this different from therapy?Therapy works on what is in the way; coaching works on where you are going
Could I just see you as a therapist instead?Warm, and still no. Different work, different rules. Offer to help them find somebody

HAND THE OBJECTION BACK

“My life is really busy.” Rather than “it’s quick and easy”: “you’ve told me how important this is — how would you make sure you had the time?”

THE SENTENCE PEOPLE FORGET

Would you like to do this? Most first conversations end well and go nowhere, because nobody ever asked.
