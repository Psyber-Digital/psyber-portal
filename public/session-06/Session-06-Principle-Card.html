Offer & Conversation

06 · Offer & Sales
A sale should be the natural outcome of a meaningful interaction. Serve, don't sell.

YOU'RE ALREADY GOOD AT THIS

In one large analysis of recorded sales calls, the ones that sold asked roughly eleven to fourteen questions, the seller talked a little less than half the time, and they went deep on three or four problems. You already work this way.

WHAT PEOPLE BUY

The changeWhat's different for them at the end. Not the list of what's included
The time it savesTwelve weeks with the wrong turns taken out, not a year of trying alone

YOUR PRICE, STEP BY STEP

Founding priceAt least $1,500, for your first three clients. A floor, not a target
ThenUp $1,000 after every three clients
The number you quoteYour price plus $500
Decide within 48 hours$500 off. It rewards a decision made, and it's already in your number
Two-way doorYou can change your price before your first client

EASIER TO SAY YES: PICK UP TO THREE, OR NONE

A genuine limitOne you would hold, like three founding places
A bonusLittle effort for you, real help for them
A guaranteeIf they come to every session, do all the work, and still aren't satisfied: a full refund

THE CALL, IN ORDER

OpenThe agenda: to see if you can help them
QuestionsWhere they are now, what they want instead, what has stopped them so far, what it costs if nothing changes
Check the fit“Are you a good fit for the program?” Answer it honestly. If not, say so, and hand them something useful. If so, ask to show them how it works
OfferTied to what they told you, then what they'll have by the end. Then your price, when they ask. If they don't, ask: “Would you like to know the investment?”
CloseWork through their concerns, then ask. A yes: book the first session, and send how to pay before you hang up. Not yet: say when the 48 hours end, in their time zone, and book a short call before then

THE PRICE MOMENT

Say the number. Then stop talking. Hold the silence: it lasts about four seconds.

CONCERNS, IN FOUR MOVES

Clarify“What do you mean by that?”
Acknowledge“That makes sense.”
GuideHelp them find their own answer: “You've told me how much this matters. How would you make sure you had the time?”
ReframeBring it back to the result they told you they want

TWO QUESTIONS YOU'LL HEAR AS A THERAPIST

How is this different from therapy?Your answer from Session 03: therapy works on what is in the way, coaching on where you are going
Could I just see you as a therapist instead?Warm, and still no. Different work, different rules. Offer to help them find a therapist other than you

THE ASK

Would you like to do this? That one question turns a good conversation into a client.

COMMON MISTAKES, AND WHAT TO DO INSTEAD

Pricing from fearPrice from the value of the result
Explaining every detailAnswer details when they ask
Giving the price too earlyGive it when they ask, or ask if they'd like to know it
Talking more than listeningAsk, then listen
Discounting on the spotHold your price. The 48-hour offer is already in it
