Foundations

Be decisive. It is better to make decisions and iterate. Decisiveness is like a muscle — the more you use it, the stronger it becomes.
Three blocks, one question: what could derail you? One focused sitting. Rough is fine: half-formed answers still count.

Beliefs

The seven that come up again and again for therapists making this move. The middle column is what each one sounds like from the inside; the right-hand column is the same person once the belief has shifted. All seven are on your Principle Card. Pick from here or write your own.
Sounds likeAn alternative belief that is true
Fixed → growthI have never coached anyone - I will be terrible at it. Who am I to charge for this?I have spent years helping people change — it is in my training, my expertise and my experience. I am well placed to coach, and with the right mindset and support I can do it well.
Unworthy → capableSomebody better could do this. There must be so many coaches with more training than me.There is no coach on the face of the earth who can deliver this coaching program the way I can. Nobody has my life experiences, background, eccentricities, style.
Scarcity → abundanceThere cannot be enough people who would pay for this. The market is saturated.There are more people who need this than I could ever serve. I only need a few of them.
Money is evil → money is neutralCharging a lot feels wrong. I did not get into this for money.Money is a tool, and what I do with it decides its character. Being paid well lifts the strain off everything else, so I can give my full self — to my family and my own life as much as to my clients.
Charge by time → charge by valueI cannot charge that - nobody will pay it. I should stick to charging by the hour, like I do with therapy.The hour is not the product. The change is the product, and that is what carries the price.
You're right → the market is rightThey just do not understand it yet. If I explain it better they will want it.I cannot talk somebody into wanting something. I can find what they already want and build for that.
Quit by feeling → adapt by feedbackI am not feeling it this week. Maybe this is not for me after all.Motivation moves every week and it is not data. I decide on what actually happened.

Circumstances

Three that derail this more often than anything in the material. None of them can be argued out of existence, so the right-hand column is what you do when one turns up rather than a truer way to see it.

Overthinking a decision that did not need itI will decide once I have thought it through properly. I just want to get it right.Set a timer for ten minutes, pick the better of two options, and write down what would make me change my mind.
A belief surfacing at the wrong momentI was about to send it, and then I thought — who is going to read this from me?Have the thought and send it anyway.
