01 · Mindset
Be decisive. It is better to make decisions and iterate. Decisiveness is like a muscle — the more you use it, the stronger it becomes.

THE QUESTION THIS SESSION ANSWERS

What could derail me on the way to becoming a successful coach? Named in advance, most of it loses its power.

THE SEVEN SHIFTS — TICK THE THREE THAT ARE LIVE FOR YOU

Fixed → growthYou have no coaching experience and decades of helping people change. The second one is what counts; the first is temporary.
Unworthy → capableSomebody better could do this. Somebody better is not sitting with your clients, hearing what you have heard, year after year.
Scarcity → abundanceThere are not enough people who would pay for this. In any real market there are far more than you could ever serve.
Money is evil → money is neutralMoney is a tool, and what you do with it decides its character. Being paid well lets you give more away, not less.
Charge by time → charge by valueThe hour is not the product. The change is the product, and the change is what carries a price.
You're right → the market is rightYou cannot make people want something. You can find what they already want, and build for that.
Quit by feeling → adapt by feedbackMotivation rises and falls every week. Decisions get made on what the market actually did.
