Launch

07 · Lead Generation
Serve, don't sell. People aren't looking for coaches; they are looking to bridge the gaps in their lives.

THE ARITHMETIC

≈8 conversations gives a good chance of one client · ≈40 replies produces 8 conversations · ≈130 personal messages produces 40 replies · a list of ≈250 reached produces 130 messages. A model rather than a measurement — we replace it with your real numbers.

LEAD OR CONNECTOR

A lead might work with you. A connector knows somebody who might. Most people are connectors, and one of them can introduce you to several.

ONE MESSAGE FOR EVERYBODY

You tell people what you are building and end by asking who they know. Easy to answer, keeps the relationship intact whatever they say — and a good number of people answer it by telling you it sounds like them.

CAST WIDE

Referrals come from anywhere. Somebody two jobs ago, a neighbour, the person who cuts your hair. Do not pre-judge who might know whom.

ONE HARD RULE

Never pay for a referral and never accept payment for one — not money, not gifts, not a percentage. ACA prohibits remuneration for referrals; NASW prohibits payment for a referral; AAMFT prohibits kickbacks, rebates and bonuses; in some states fee-splitting is unlawful. General business advice on paid referral programs is not written for licensed practitioners.

THE RHYTHM

Ten personal messages a day, about twenty minutes, four days a week. The whole list in six weeks. Follow up twice — three days, then ten, each shorter than the last.

START WHERE THE TRUST IS

Face to face → video → voice → message → email. Your twenty warmest people get a voice note or a call.

THE WALL, AND WHAT GETS YOU THROUGH IT

You will have the list open and the message written and not send it. That is completely normal. Shrink it to five, to the people whose good opinion is least at stake — and send the first ones on the call, together.
