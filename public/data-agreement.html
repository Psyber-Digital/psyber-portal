Data Processing Agreement for Build™ and Build Free members

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Between: EdInfinitum Ltd, trading as Psyber Digital, company number 13859238, registered office 71-75 Shelton Street, London, England, WC2H 9JQ ("Psyber Digital", "we", "us"), and the member who accepted the Build membership terms ("you").

This agreement forms part of those membership terms (clause 8.1).

1. Two different relationships, kept apart

The portal handles two kinds of personal data, and the law treats them differently.

a) Data we hold about you. Your name, email, payment record and program progress. For this we are the controller: we decide why we hold it. How we handle it is described in our privacy policy at psyberdigital.com/privacy-policy. Sections 2 to 9 of this agreement do not govern it.

b) Data you hold about other people. The contact list you build inside the portal: the people you intend to approach about your coaching program. For this you are the controller and we are your processor. You decide who goes in it and why. We only store it and make it available to you. Sections 2 to 9 govern this.

2. Scope of our processing

We process the personal data in your contact list only to provide the portal: storing it, showing it back to you, and keeping it available and secure.

  • People on the list: people you choose to add, such as former colleagues, professional acquaintances, friends, and anyone else you decide to approach. Fellow clinicians are not a referral channel for your coaching (terms 1.3).
  • Personal data: name, email address, phone number, the categories and statuses you assign, contact dates, and anything you write in the notes field.
  • How long: for as long as your portal account exists, or until you delete the entries.

3. What we will do

  • Act only on your instructions. We process this data only to provide the portal, and for no purpose of our own. We will not use your contact list for our own marketing, will not add it to our email tools, and will not export it anywhere.
  • Keep it confidential. Anyone with access is bound by confidentiality.
  • Secure it. The measures are set out in section 5.
  • Help you meet your obligations. If one of your contacts asks you for their data, asks you to delete it, or objects, we will help you respond. You can already do all of this yourself in the portal.
  • Tell you about breaches without undue delay (section 7).
  • Delete it when you ask, or when your account is closed (section 8).
  • Let you check. On reasonable notice, we will give you the information you need to satisfy yourself that we are meeting this agreement.

4. What you agree to

  • You are the controller. You decide who goes on the list and why. You are responsible for having a proper reason to hold their details and to contact them.
  • You will comply with the law that applies to you. Depending on where you and your contacts are, that may include US state privacy laws, CAN-SPAM, UK GDPR or EU GDPR, and your own professional and ethical obligations.
  • You will keep coaching and therapy separate. See section 6.
  • You authorize our sub-processors in section 9.

5. Security

We protect your contact list with:

  • Row-level security in the database, which limits every row to your own account.
  • No administrator path. The contacts table has no administrator access rule. There is no screen in our admin pages, and no part of the portal, that can read your contact list.
  • EU hosting of the database. The portal database is hosted in Ireland (EU).
  • Encryption in transit. Passwords are stored only in hashed form. We never see your password.

To be accurate: like any hosted system, we hold an administrative database key that can bypass those row-level limits. What this section promises is that no part of the product gives us a route to your contact list, and that under section 3 we will not use that key to read it. It is a commitment plus a design control. It is not a claim that access is technically impossible.

6. What must not go into the portal

You must not enter any of the following into any field:

  • Clinical records, case notes or health information about anyone.
  • Anything that shows a person was your therapy client. You may add former therapy clients by name and contact details only, as connectors who might know someone. You never record that they were your client, or anything about their care, in any field or note (terms 8.3).
  • Other special-category data about any person.
  • Anything you are professionally or legally prohibited from sharing with a third party.

If you enter something in this category, tell us and we will delete it. This protects your professional position as much as ours.

7. Breaches

If we become aware of a breach affecting your contact list, we will tell you without undue delay, with what we know: what happened, what data, the likely consequences, and what we have done. Any decision about notifying a regulator or the people affected is yours to make as the controller. We will give you what you need to make it and to meet your own deadlines.

8. Deletion and export

You can delete any entry at any time in the portal. You can export your whole list from the portal at any time. When your account is closed, your contact list is deleted with it. Export it first if you want a copy.

If your paid membership ends, your list stays with you on Build Free (terms 8.2).

9. Sub-processors

We use these sub-processors for this data:

Sub-processor Role Location
Supabase Database hosting and sign-in Ireland, European Union
Vercel Runs the portal's web pages. Your list passes through it while you use the portal. It does not keep a copy. United States

Your contact list is not shared with any other provider. That includes our email tools, our outreach tools and any analytics. We will give you reasonable notice before adding or changing a sub-processor for this data, and you may object.

10. General

This agreement runs for as long as we hold your contact list. Where it conflicts with the membership terms on data protection, this agreement takes precedence. It is governed by the law of England and Wales.
